    Why is so much of the map green? For farm & construction work, almost everywhere outside the capital cities qualifies. The hatched zones — Sydney, Melbourne, Brisbane, Perth, Adelaide — are the main places it doesn’t count.

    How this works & important caveats
    You can already work anywhere. A 462 visa lets you do almost any job anywhere in Australia for 12 months (max 6 months per employer). These colours only matter for extending to a 2nd or 3rd year.

    To get a 2nd 462 visa you need 3 months (88 days) of specified work; for a 3rd, 6 months (179 days) — done in the eligible areas and industries shown here.

    The area decides the job. Tourism & hospitality only counts in Remote/Northern areas; plant & animal cultivation and construction count in Regional/Northern; fishing, pearling and tree-felling count in Northern Australia only.

    Boundaries are ABS Postal Area (POA) 2021 approximations of postcodes — they don’t perfectly match Australia Post boundaries, and a few PO-box-only postcodes have no area on the map. Always confirm against the official page before relying on it.
